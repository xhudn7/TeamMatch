About Us

Team Match is a platform designed for university students to find the perfect teammates for their graduation projects. Our mission is to connect students based on skills, interests, and project ideas to ensure successful collaboration.

Our Vision

To make it easy for students to form strong, effective teams and complete their graduation projects successfully while gaining valuable experience.

Our Team

Team Match was created by a group of passionate university students who understand the challenges of finding the right teammates.
